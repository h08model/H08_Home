H08 Water Risk Tool

We are developing a viewer that displays the world's water risk index based on the latest simulation results from H08. There are two types of viewers: the Water Security Compass (WSC) and the H08 Water Risk Tool (WRT). The WSC was developed by the Research Initiative for Global Hydrologic Cycles at the University of Tokyo, and we recommend using this viewer unless there are particular circumstances. The WRT is a visualization of the results submitted by H08 to the international project Inter Sectoral Impact Model Intercomparison Project (ISIMIP). It has the advantage that the water risk indicators displayed correspond to the data on the ISIMIP Repository, but it is not recommended for the general use.
